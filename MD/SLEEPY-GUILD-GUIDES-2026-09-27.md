# Sleepy guild boss guide source audit (2026-09-27)

Source: [Sleepy's Guild Boss Guides playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL0Y9I56KZ1tNMgwxYfaU_V70C910MPAvN), 51 public entries when inspected. `src/sleepy-guides.js` records each title, game version, named bosses, mode, playlist position, and exact video link. The Team Preview target card displays the three newest matching routes for its boss and mode.

The newest [version 4.9.2 guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=27eceES5cjQ&list=PL0Y9I56KZ1tNMgwxYfaU_V70C910MPAvN&index=1&pp=iAQB) identifies a Multidimensional Dreamscape route for Yatsufusa and Surt. Its description lists Yatsufusa at 0:00, Surt at 10:41, and team details at 17:33 and 18:47. It names Cosmic Yui, Beachflower Marian, Twins, and Miku for Yatsufusa; Wavecatcher Puppet, Cherish, Tempest Wind, and Ange for Surt. The exported auto captions are Japanese battle voice lines, not an explanation of boss rules. Video playback did not provide viewable frames in the inspection environment. The route and roster details are thus references, not a verified combat trace.

The existing 2.3 simulator has a Surt encounter with separately sourced live mechanics and a Hachiman encounter. This change adds source discovery for those encounters and other represented bosses. It does **not** modify damage, score, timing, enemy AI, or boss data. The playlist title alone cannot establish those values. Yatsufusa remains absent from selectable bosses; adding it requires readable in-game effect panels, an action-by-action battle log or clear footage, and measured outputs. In particular, its HP/Defense, mode effects, summons, berserk behavior, turn order, and scoring cannot be inferred from the latest guide title or voice-line captions.

For each future mechanic, record the video timestamp, relevant in-game text or observed state transition, current engine behavior, proposed rule, and a replay assertion against a game log. Older guides cover different game versions, so treat any conflicting rule as version-specific until confirmed for the current encounter.
