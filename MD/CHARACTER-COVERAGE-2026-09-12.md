# Character research and module coverage

Updated September 12, 2026. Covers all 26 existing combatant and navigator entries. No bosses or additional roster entries were added.

All four research/implementation workers were switched from inherited GPT-6 to GPT-5.6 Terra at the user's request, retaining their source snapshots and draft files. Integration and review remained in the main task.

## Result

- Dedicated data and mechanics modules for 14 previously generic/base entries, including navigators.
- 33 existing methods extracted into 10 character-specific modules, with source-identity verification and regression tests.
- Cosmic Yui retained as the reference implementation; Wonder's Persona and weapon systems retained separately.
- Shared per-target damage, cast, action, turn, navigator and extra-action hooks connected to the engine.
- Build-screen source options and expandable research/limitations panels. Recorded-profile hooks remain disabled.
- 308 tests passed at handoff, including fast/full deterministic parity, real battle steps, multi-hit accounting and extra-action timing checks.

This is **not full-kit completion for every character**. Sources are community-maintained game-data snapshots plus the existing observed evidence. Tests establish the specified simulator behavior, not universal live-game accuracy. Unsupported actions and uncertain mechanics remain explicitly limited.
