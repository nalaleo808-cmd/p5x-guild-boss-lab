# P5X live calibration controls

Verified in the live Surt MLD run on 2026-09-19.

## Reliable controls

- Select an offensive skill from the character or Persona menu, then click Surt's on-field model to execute it.
- The score display updates after the action. Record the display before and after an isolated action to preserve a usable score delta.
- Clicking a party portrait outside target selection opens its runtime panel. This exposed the current Wonder and Beachflower Minami level, HP, SP, affinities, and Surt field effects.
- The Surt panel confirms: Ragnarok stacks increase damage by 5% and remove HP each turn, party Ice damage increases by 20%, and party Attack plus Resonance critical damage each increase by 25%.

## Party-target limitation

Universal Theoria entered party-target selection, but the current PC build did not confirm a target through:

- Clicking Beachflower Minami's on-field model.
- Cycling targets with `A` and `D`, then clicking the selected model or battle floor.
- Clicking the party portrait or the on-screen Confirm prompt.

None of those attempts consumed SP, advanced the action count, or changed the score. Keep the rotation trace at its confirmed first action until the game accepts direct ally targeting or a working keybind is supplied by the player.
